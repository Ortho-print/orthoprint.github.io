Resins designed for appliances worn every day

Most printable resins used for aligners, retainers and splints were adapted from general dental or industrial materials. ORTHOPRINT builds the knowledge needed to design them for orthodontics from the start.

Aim

From eleven formulations to transferable design rules

Each formulation must pass three decision gates. Those that fail a predefined threshold are not advanced unchanged, which keeps the results interpretable and the scope under control.

  1. 11 formulations

    Year 1

    Urethane-acrylate and hybrid systems within a bounded design space.

  2. Gate 1

    End of year 1

    Printability and degree-of-conversion thresholds.

  3. Gate 2

    Year 2

    At least 80% retention of key mechanical properties after aging.

  4. Gate 3

    Year 3

    Chemical safety and at least 70% cell viability (ISO 10993-5).

  5. Design rules

    Year 3

    Processing envelopes validated on at least two printing platforms.

    Three years, four work packages

    Work packages overlap so that each one receives the formulations that passed the previous gate as soon as they are selected.
